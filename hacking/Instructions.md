# How to Play Asteroids

## Controls
- **W**: Move Up
- **A**: Move Left
- **S**: Move Down
- **D**: Move Right
- **Spacebar**: Fire weapon
- **q**: Quit the game

## Objective
- Survive as long as possible.
- Destroy asteroids to get points.
- Destroy other players to get a portion of their points.
- Collect health packs to heal.

## Leveling Up
- Gain experience by scoring points.
- When you level up, your weapon will be randomly upgraded.
